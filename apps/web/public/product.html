Know before you connect

Your tenant.
Your decision.

The online service processes configuration on its server. Results are retained for 30 days and audit events for 90 days. Downloaded reports remain under your control.

The online service records anonymous daily usage counts on its server: assessments started, completed and failed (by a fixed reason code), which collectors returned data, controls evaluated, report downloads by format, and distinct organisations, counted as a salted one-way hash. No tenant ID, domain, user ID or finding is stored with the counts, nothing is sent to a third party, and the counts are deleted after about 13 months (400 days).

The separate fictional-sample edition runs locally in your browser. An assessment is not certification or a guarantee of security.
